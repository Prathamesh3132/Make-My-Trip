# 🧳 MakeMyTrip Customer Insight Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides deep insights into customer behavior, booking trends, hotel preferences, and pricing patterns for MakeMyTrip – a leading travel booking platform. The dashboard is designed for data-driven decision-making across travel planning, customer support, and business strategy.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives
- Understand customer satisfaction through ratings and reviews
- Analyze booking trends by destination, room type, and season
- Monitor pricing and discounts over time
- Identify top-performing destinations and transport modes
- Evaluate customer loyalty and support ratings

---

## 📁 Dataset Highlights
The dashboard was built using a simulated travel dataset with the following tables:
- **Bookings** – Contains booking IDs, destination, transport mode, and room type
- **Customers** – Includes loyalty points, number of travelers, and support ratings
- **Hotels** – Holds hotel ratings and review comments
- **Transactions** – Captures prices, discounts, and payment status

## 💡 Key Takeaways
- Goa is the highest-priced and most flight-included destination
- Deluxe and Standard rooms contribute the highest revenue
- Customer satisfaction varies by review category, with "Excellent" and "Good" comments linked to higher loyalty
- Peak bookings and revenue occur between February–May
